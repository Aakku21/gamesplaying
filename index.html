❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to common questions about playing Urban Escape:

What is Urban Escape: Boy vs Police?
Urban Escape is a free, instant-play endless runner web game. You play as a street boy escaping a police officer across dynamic city rooftops and streets, jumping over barricades, sliding under overhead pipes, and collecting coins to boost speed.
How do you jump over obstacles in the road?
Press the Up Arrow (▲), W key, or Spacebar on your keyboard. On smartphones and tablets, simply tap the on-screen JUMP button or swipe up.
How do you slide under overhead caution blockers?
Press the Down Arrow (▼) or S key on your keyboard, or tap the on-screen SLIDE button on mobile. The boy will slide along the asphalt, easily dodging overhead signs and pipes.
Why does the boy slow down when hitting a blocker?
Hitting any road or overhead blocker causes the boy to stumble, reducing his speed to 3.4 units. When the boy slows down, the police officer surges forward to catch him. Running cleanly afterwards restores your safe lead!
How does collecting coins increase running speed?
Every coin collected grants a permanent +0.15 speed bonus. As you bank more points, your boy naturally sprints faster and faster, opening up a commanding lead over the police officer.
How do I get the skateboard?
You can get the skateboard in two ways: by picking up the glowing 🛹 skateboard power-up spawned along the track, or automatically every time you collect 12 coins.
Is Urban Escape free and unblocked?
Yes, Urban Escape is 100% free, requires no login or installation, and is unblocked for smooth play on school, college, work, and home networks.
